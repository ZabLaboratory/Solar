## Result

Persistent Vision sessions now record engine readiness separately from installation-font readiness. If `init-empty` fails before the `engine-ready` receipt, Solar removes that unusable session and retires its presenter and hidden layer; the next prewarm creates a fresh presenter. If the engine is ready and font admission fails, Solar keeps the presenter and retries only fonts that were not acknowledged.

The generated Vision presenter confirms that `init-empty` emits `engine-ready` after engine initialization. The unit test mock exercises both recovery paths and verifies that failed initialization does not retain a layer or presenter, while font retry reuses the same presenter and engine.

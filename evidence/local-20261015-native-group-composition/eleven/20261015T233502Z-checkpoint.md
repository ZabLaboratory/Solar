## Behavior covered

`NativeCompositionSession` now records the latest accepted active document, projection, and exact source state hash even while its composition peer is absent. A replacement publisher sends that one current plan, or the current clear state, without waiting for another LSML mutation. The live primary LSML/Vision session remains the source of accepted documents; the side channel retains no scene history and creates no LSML receipt.

A terminal composition socket close is reported and the unusable publisher is discarded without trying to clear over the dead peer. The existing runtime reconnect scheduler obtains a fresh token and replaces only that peer. Recovery resets shared backoff only after the current replacement publisher completes its current plan or clear. Primary reconnect retains priority, and stop/token-refresh fences prevent a pending composition token result from being admitted into a newer lifetime.

The new `native-composition-session.test.ts` uses the real session and publisher with a deterministic fake LSDP peer: it publishes scene A, closes only the composition peer, accepts scene B while offline, then restarts without another mutation and checks that the resource contains only B with B's exact `stateHash` and projection viewport. It also verifies visible close-error reporting, recovery completion, and retirement cleanup. Separate scheduler cases cover primary retry priority, no backoff reset on peer construction, token-refresh invalidation, and stop/start stale-token rejection.
